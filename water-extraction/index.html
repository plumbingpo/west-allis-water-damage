Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Across West Allis, West Allis Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.
