Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.

How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
